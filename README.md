# Non-Agricultural-Enterprises-Analysis
**Project Overview**
   This project aim is to analysis the Urban vs Rural development in India on the basis of Enterprises type other than agriculture based on the 2016 Enterprises report to improve the India's economy using Excel       and PowerBI. 

**Objectives**
   The main objective is to demonstrate and visualize the Enterprises type, employee of the enterprises based on sector and state to the Urban and Rural development in India based on the 2016 Enterprises report.

**Tools & Technologies**
    Power BI – Data modeling, visualization, and dashboard creation • Excel / CSV – Data source and preprocessing

**Key Insights**
    Goa has the highest gva (gross value added) and Sikkim has the lowest gva to Indian Economy towards non-agricultural Enterprises in the year of 2016.
    Male has the major propriety ownership when compared to female, “partnership of same gender” and “partnership of different gender” at India in the year of 2016
    Kerala state in Urban sector (4.88%) has major propriety ownership when compared to Rural (3.64%) and urban and rural combined (4.39 %) sector towards India.
   Micro MSME (504 M) count is greater than small MSME (3 M), Medium MSME (42 k) and non MSME (25k).

**Business Value**
    This dashboard helps organizations: working hours of the employess in the Enterprises,Communiuty wise Enterprises,Act used by Enterpises,state and gender wise properitary.

**Conclusion**
   This analysis demonstrates the Urban vs Rural based enterprises other than agriculture on a state and sector basis. The Power BI dashboard shows the sum of MSME (micro, small, minimum enterprises) and non MSME,    sum of community wise enterprises, gva, propriety and government act on state wise, employee working hours in the enterprises to improve the Rural based enterprises and to improve the women propriety (ownership   of the enterprises) in India.
